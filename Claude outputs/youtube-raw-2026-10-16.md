Short: price breaks below OR low and stays below VWAP, wait for pullback toward VWAP/moving averages, entry on bearish engulfing candle; stop above VWAP; long = mirror. Skip names inside the range. Risk: daily budget (example Rs 5,000) split Rs 1,000 per trade, max 3-4 concurrent. Target is NOT specified in the transcript, so incomplete.

S2. 3-min Heikin-Ashi + 100 EMA pullback scalp on Nifty (Dhan, JchD2CK6dM0). Window 10:00-12:00 only. Bias: HA candles above 100 EMA = long only, below = short only. Wait for a counter-trend pullback; skip the first 2-3 candles that turn back; entry when a doji/spinning top forms, with a buy-stop above its high (short: sell-stop below its low). SL = doji low (short: doji high); target equals the SL distance (example 10 pts risk, 10 pts target); aim for 10-20 trades/day. Fully testable; costs/STT matter at 10-pt targets (speaker concedes).
