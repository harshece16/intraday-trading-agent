**Not run.** This is step 1 of the brief and is pending implementation.

stop-sizing logic**, over **≥200 seeds**, reporting the distribution rather

Expected result, restated for the reviewer: gross expectancy near zero, and a

have been made. The method is now **settled** (amendment 7): Benjamini–Hochberg

## 10. Carried caveats

These apply to every number in §2 and to every result phase 2 will produce:

- **Survivorship bias.** The universe is today's NIFTY 100 applied to
  history; stocks that left the index, usually by falling, are absent. This
  flatters long-only results — so the true figures are likely *worse* than
  reported, not better.
- **Backtest picks use daily data only.** The live scanner additionally
  applies gap, relative-volume, news and results-day filters that need
  intraday context and are not replayed in backtests.
- **Fill convention differs from live.** The backtester fills at the signal
  candle's close; the live engine fills a few seconds later at the live price
  (issue #13).
- **The harness is unvalidated** until step 1 runs (§4).
