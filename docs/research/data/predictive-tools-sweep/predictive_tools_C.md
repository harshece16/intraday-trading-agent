## Candidates

### 1. Cross-sectional overnight-gap reversal (H2) and the `nq tilt` candidate — ullasgh/nifty-intraday-quant-research
The most carefully built India-specific intraday research repo found (1-minute bars, 149 NSE equities + 4 index/VIX series, 2017-07 → 2026-08, 121M bars; causal-guarded features; CSCV/PBO and deflated Sharpe wired; last commit 2026-08-31). Its five pre-registered hypotheses killed index morning→afternoon momentum (0.09 bp), intraday cross-sectional reversal (+1.21 bp, and the sign is momentum), volatility compression (−6.26 bp, sign reversed, regime-alternating) and an F&O OI-change conditioner (−7.27 bp, decays to −2.4 bp in 2025; a deliberate same-day-lookahead variant gave −49 bp, a 6.7× leak multiple worth remembering). The one survivor is H2: rank each name by last night's close-to-open return; at 10:00 the top decile of overnight losers reverses by −24.30 bp by 15:20 (t −16.66, 1,867 sessions, 8/8 years). But the effect has decayed to −10.98 bp (2024) and −9.62 bp (2025), and to −3.04 bp on continuous-coverage names, so it fails their own two-leg hurdle at every clip size in recent years; the recent edge is carried by 18 stocks that IPO'd inside the window. The long-only low-turnover tilt (a=0.10) nets +2.29 bp/day pooled and is positive in all eight years, but its 2025 figure is +0.84 bp/day, its recent-window significance flips on the SE estimator (t 2.75 iid, 3.20 NW; continuous subset p 0.046–0.077), the edge sits in the bottom-two ADV deciles, and their Phase G3 (2026-08-31) found no weighting scheme promotes at DSR 0.95. The holdout is unread. Relevance: the in-house overnight work is the time-series ETF hold (trials 124–125) and the ATR-sorted decomposition (L23–L25, L27–L28); the cross-sectional rank of the gap itself is not in the tested list. Against the project's bar it cannot pass — a full-session decile spread of ~10 bp (≈3 bp like-for-like) will not be 33 bp inside 60 minutes — so the honest recommendation is skip, or one measurement look at the 09:45 decision time only (feature: prev bhavcopy close → 09:45 close; 60-min target; partial IC given m60) to close the line on record.

### 2. The Accuracy Illusion — rachitranka25/accuracy-illusion
New (created 2026-09-14), NSE-specific, and the best-argued negative found: fourteen months of NIFTY/BANKNIFTY/FINNIFTY minute data, nine model families under purged walk-forward with shuffled-label and injected-signal controls, 86 rule strategies with block-bootstrap nulls, and a decade of EOD option chains. Direction: the best per-bar 51.3% (p 0.001) becomes 50.4% (p 0.69) when scored one observation per outcome bar; break-even needs 71.5% at 4 bp; at ATM option costs no accuracy suffices. The author retracts five of the project's own earlier positives (71% dashboard accuracy, ORB "+EV" that is −18.6R over 351 sessions, PCR extremes, a "stable 60% breakout" that is PF 0.95 after costs). What survives: volatility magnitude 68–74% OOS (matches the in-house "sizing input only" verdict); EOD option-chain residuals with IC 0.04–0.06 over 2,181 days, where the change in PCR beats its level; and one open lead, a fixed OI-cluster pinning fade on BANKNIFTY at 60% and +0.211%/trade over 55 OOS days, which the author correctly refuses to call a result. The transferable point is the counting correction: with persistent calls and overlapping outcomes, n_eff = n·E[L]/E[L²] (≈6 per 69-forecast session); the project's per-session IC is immune, but any per-bar hit-rate the dashboard ever shows is not. Skip as a signal source; cite.

### 3. Vaunox/quant-intraday
A closed program on exactly the project's instrument and clock: liquid NSE cash equities, MIS, 15-minute decisions, gradient-boosted models on point-in-time features, Kite Connect. OOS AUC 0.520 (lgbm 0.529), kill-gate net CPCV Sharpe −0.977 at 17–21 bp round trip; 60-minute bars −0.90; 5/1-minute features no lift; daily 56-name cross-section +0.148 (t≈1.66 de-duplicated); cointegration pairs +0.425 net Sharpe, insignificant, "the strongest edge the project ever found"; monthly factors IC +0.051; an external "60% / +0.662 Sharpe" claim audited down to +0.148. Zero capital risked. It corroborates trials 41–116 and §69. Intraday pairs on NSE cash is the one line here the project has not touched; the evidence for it is a sub-threshold, insignificant Sharpe, which is not a reason to spend a trial.

### 4. NSE bulk/block-deal disclosures
India-specific and uncovered by the sweep. The literature is event-study, gross, in-sample: Rajvanshi & Paul (2022; NSE 2010–2019) find 5–7% abnormal return within a week around bulk deals, mostly before the event (front-running), higher for buys; Chaturvedula et al. (2015; 2004–2012) find significant CARs; Agarwalla & Pandey (IIMA WP 2010-04-02; 500 stocks, 1-minute data) find block purchases carry ~1.6% permanent impact while block sales revert, with prices rising 8 minutes before block purchases in the tape. Disclosure is after 17:00, so the tradable window is the next open. The project holds bhavcopies and 5-minute NIFTY 100 bars; the public deal archive is downloadable. Expect the same shape L36 found for results (priced in the open, +5.9 bp at 30 min, 0 by close), and few events inside NIFTY 100. Later, as a single event look after the open lines close, and only if n ≥ 60.

### 5. MFAST — arXiv 2609.23703 (Kirtac, 2026-09-20)
A framework paper for news-based trading that jointly tests event-time observability, calibration, execution timing, transaction costs, liquidity, capacity and inference, with a leakage control the project should copy (evaluate only on news after the foundation model's disclosed data-freshness date). US data (Refinitiv News Analytics + CRSP); decoder-only LMs beat encoder and dictionary baselines net of costs; no headline figure in the abstract; single author, revise-and-resubmit. Not India, not intraday, and the in-house LLM/sentiment family already failed on broker-verified evidence. Later, only if an NSE announcement corpus with timestamps exists (the XBRL feed behind L36 is the seed).

### 6. GIFT Nifty implied open
The only academic result found is price discovery, not prediction: JRFM 18(9):527 (Sept 2025) shows cointegration, bidirectional Granger causality and that GIFT Nifty leads the Nifty index (Hasbrouck IS / Gonzalo–Granger CS). Blog claims of 75–90% "accuracy" describe the implied open, which the 09:00–09:08 auction absorbs; nobody publishes a costed post-open continuation or fade result. The project has no GIFT Nifty history (NSE IX, not Kite). Skip.

### 11. Indian text datasets
NifSent50 (Kaggle) labels each headline by the subsequent >1% move, so it cannot be used to predict that move; daily, no minute timestamps. The Multi-Channel Sentiment Corpus (S&P 500 + Nifty 500 speaker-role-segmented earnings-call transcripts and headlines, 2026-07) is a corpus, not a result. Later, only as material for a PEAD (L39) follow-up.

## Bottom line
Nothing found is a "test" at the project's bar. The one genuinely India-specific effect the project has not measured in its own form — the cross-sectional overnight-gap reversal — is documented by an independent, careful repo at a size (~10 bp/session in 2024–25, ~3 bp like-for-like) that cannot reach 33 bp in an hour. Every new-since-09-20 item is either US, uncosted, or a corroborating negative. The most useful outputs of this sweep are two external negatives on the project's exact instrument (Vaunox; accuracy-illusion) and one counting rule worth adopting for any hit-rate display.
