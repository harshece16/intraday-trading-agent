CORRECTION (verified in code by main session): the paper book ranks the 200 most-traded NSE EQ stocks (universe 200, liquidity 126 sessions, min price Rs 20), holds top 15, keeps while rank <= 30; benchmark = equal-weighted universe. NOT the NIFTY 100. Restricting to NIFTY 100 would be a new config; large-cap evidence says it would lower excess.
Bottom line: momentum well replicated, not decayed outside US; plan for live well below backtest. Long-short enhancements give a long-only book ~half, often less. Gate so strict that signal/construction tweaks rarely pay for the trial. Best return per trial: measuring and reducing TAX (not modelled; ~4-6x modelled trading cost); next: one pre-registered RISK OVERLAY judged on a risk rule agreed before data.
0 Gate: trial 126 +0.71%/mo, sd 3.89%, ~190 months, t 2.52, monthly IR 0.1825. Family bar (two-sided Bonferroni normal approx): t 2.807 at N=10, 2.84 N=11, 2.87 N=12, 2.94 N=15. Base rule already fails N=10 bar. New config must beat base IR by +13% (N=11) to +17% (N=15), e.g. 0.80%/mo at sd 3.89 or 0.71% at sd <=3.43. Paired tests underpowered: half-name swap diff sd ~2%/mo -> real +0.15%/mo gives t ~0.9 over 190 months, ~1.2 over 288; detectable at 80% power ~0.41%/mo (diff sd 2%) or ~0.20% (diff sd 1%). Overlay diff sd ~0.9%/mo -> needs ~+2.0%/yr for t 2.5 over 190 months, +1.6%/yr over 290. Literature long-only gains mostly 0-0.2%/mo.
